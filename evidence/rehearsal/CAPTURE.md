# GATE-L1 rehearsal capture (V-5)

The plan asked for an internal screen recording of the attack, the DENY and the unchanged live list before the final demo was recorded. The stills in this folder are that rehearsal: `S1-delete-prod.png` (the delete attempt), `S2c-deny.png` and `S2d-prod-still-denied.png` (the DENY and prod still protected), `T1-ledger.png` (the ledger row) and the S5 and S6 runaway and dollar-ceiling runs.

The moving version is the final demo video, `plumbline-demo.mp4` (sha256 8135b9d0..., 4 minutes 38 seconds), which shows the same sequence live. The release asset is at https://github.com/dmustapha/plumbline/releases/tag/demo-video and is not committed to the repo.

No separate pre-demo screen recording exists. The stills and the final video stand in for it.
